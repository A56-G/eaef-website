( The plant )

Inside
the plant

Nine stops from billet to delivery, across both plants. Every photo here was taken on our own floor.

01

Billet store

Aluminum billets wait in racks, sorted by alloy, before they are heated.

02

Extrusion

An 8-inch press in Plant 1, and two 6-inch presses and a 10-inch press in Plant 2, push the hot billet through the die.

03

At the controls

Operators set speed, temperature and length for every die from the press console.

04

Runout & stretch

Profiles leave the press on Tecalex and Turla runout tables to cool, stretch and be cut to length.

05

Die shop

Dies are prepared and corrected in our own workshops in both plants.

06

Powder coating

A horizontal line in Plant 1 and a vertical line in Plant 2, with Jotun powders in the full RAL range.

07

Thermal break

Knurling and crimping lock polyamide strips between two aluminum sections in Plant 2.

08

Quality checks

Dimensions, coating thickness and adhesion are checked in our labs before anything ships.

09

Packed & shipped

Profiles are wrapped, labeled and sent across the Kingdom, the GCC and out through Dammam Port.

( Thermal break )

Cool in summer. Warm in winter.

Aluminum is strong and light, but it carries heat. A polyamide strip between the outer and inner frame breaks that path and damps sound too.

  • up to1,000×Less heat conducted than aluminum
  • PolyamideStrip material

New · Trial operation
2026 · Expansion

Our fourth press, a 10-inch line, starts trial operation

We have started trial operations of our fourth aluminum extrusion press, a 10-inch line. It adds capacity, strengthens our position in a growing market and supports Saudi Vision 2030 goals for local manufacturing and non-oil exports.

Read the full announcement

We are proud to announce the commencement of trial operations of our fourth 10-inch aluminum extrusion press, a significant milestone that marks another step forward in our journey toward industrial excellence, sustainable growth, and market leadership.

This strategic expansion represents more than an increase in production capacity. It reflects our commitment to strengthening our competitive position, enhancing our ability to serve a growing market, and reinforcing our presence among the leading aluminum manufacturers in the region.

With this new addition, we are further strengthening our operational capabilities, creating greater opportunities to meet evolving customer demands, and laying the foundation for continued growth and long-term success.

Beyond our business ambitions, this milestone aligns with the broader aspirations of Saudi Vision 2030. By expanding local manufacturing capabilities, we contribute to the Kingdom's industrial development, support the growth of domestic production, and advance the national ambition to increase non-oil exports and diversify the economy.

We believe that a stronger industrial base is essential to building a more competitive, resilient, and diversified economy, one driven by innovation, local capabilities, and value-added manufacturing.

As we move forward, this milestone reinforces our ambition to grow beyond capacity, compete beyond borders, and contribute meaningfully to the future of Saudi Arabia's aluminum industry.

A new press. Greater capabilities. Stronger competitiveness. A bigger contribution to the Kingdom's industrial future.
